# Rust checks of "Shortest isogenies between supersingular elliptic curves with complex multiplication" (2026-11-27)

No claim of the paper failed. Every assertion passes on branch `agent/shortiso`. Four things differ from what was asked or expected, the most important first. Points 2 and 3 were settled on 2026-11-27, after the first version of this report; item 7 was added the same day.

1. The crate's `ideal_to_isogeny_qlapoty` returns `None` on I = O0 theta + O0 m at all three SQIsign levels: 0 of 8 attempts each, on verified equivalent ideals of odd prime norm. This is neither a bug in the check nor an error in the paper. Qlapoty starts from the smallest ideal of the class and needs its norm odd, and the crate's `shortest_equivalent` returns norm exactly m (2^162, 2^250, 2^331): the paper's minimality seen from the crate. Two checks that pass replace it: `kernel_dlogs_to_ideal_even` on the generator of G returns I itself, and `ideal_to_isogeny_qlapoty` on I ∩ L (L random of odd prime norm) gives the j-invariant of E_L / phi_L(G) computed on the curve side.
2. The two-condition statements for D = 7 and 28 were not in the request, so I reconstructed them. D = 7: least delta with 7 delta^2 - p or 7 delta^2 - 2p a square. D = 28: least delta with 28 delta^2 - p or 7 delta^2 - p a square. Both hold with no exception. Settled: these are the statements of the paper's two propositions, word for word in the conditions. (The pair 28 delta^2 - p, 28 delta^2 - 8p would fail at 19290 of 54260 primes, first at p = 47; it is kept in the test as a control.)
3. "The three SQIsign primes are also proposed for MIKE" could not be confirmed from here: the MIKE repositories list six primes and none of the SQIsign ones, and the MIKE paper (ePrint 2026/2377) could not be fetched (HTTP 403). Settled by the author: Appendix J.4 of the MIKE paper, whose item 2 reuses the SQIsign primes and whose items 1 and 3 give the other six. Not read here.
4. `iso_isogeny_2chain_from(curve, K, length)` walks length - 2 steps, as the crate's own callers assume; its doc comment says "2^length-isogeny". The check gives it a point two doublings above the generator of G.

## What was checked and how

- Item 1, Corollary A at 3*2^324-1, 27*2^500-1, 17*2^664-1, on the crate's field, curve and basis of E0[2^f]. The only stored matrix used is `ACTION_GEN3`, the action of (iota + pi)/2; for odd D the scalar m/2 is subtracted. On E0[m] its determinant is 0 mod m and it has an odd entry; on the curve the image of P has order exactly m and the image of Q is the predicted multiple of it, so G is cyclic of order m. The chain was walked by single 2-isogenies, by the crate's 4-isogeny step, and by its strategy-based chain; the three agree. Endpoints: j = 0 after 162 steps with j = 54000 at step 161; j = -12288000 after 250 steps; a root of H_-68 mod p after 331 steps. All 163, 251 and 332 j-invariants have zero imaginary part and are pairwise distinct, the starting 1728 included.
- H_-68 = X^4 - 178211040000 X^3 - 75843692160000000 X^2 - 318507038720000000000 X - 2089297506304000000000000 (`classpoly/H_-68.txt`). It was computed in Rust from the four reduced forms of discriminant -68 by q-expansions of E4 and Delta in fixed-point arithmetic, rounded with the error checked below 2^-64. The same code reproduces the 13 class-number-one j-invariants and H_-15, H_-20, H_-23. No Sage was used.
- Item 2, quaternion side, same primes. I is built by `create_o0_pow_two` and again from its eight generators; the two lattices are equal. Its index in O0 is m^2, the gcd of its norms is m, and it is not inside 2 O0. Its right order (basis in `endpoints/level{1,3,5}.txt`) is a ring, stabilises I and has the covolume of a maximal order. It contains theta/m, of trace t and norm (D + t)/4, so of discriminant -D; at D = 27 the order of discriminant -3 is not in it, and at D = 3 and 68 no larger order exists.
- Item 3, generic F_{p^2} on num-bigint (already a dev-dependency), Jacobian points, iota and Frobenius on points, Velu. At the three SQIsign primes it gives the same kernel points as the stored matrix and the same chains value for value. The chain check then passes on the 6 MIKE primes (endpoints are roots of H_-468, H_-308, H_-164, H_-2532, H_-2372, H_-1268), on the 15 primes 3*2^f-1 with f even below 500 (f = 2, 4, 6, 18, 34, 38, 64, 76, 94, 206, 216, 306, 324, 458, 470), on the 12 Mersenne primes with 5 <= f <= 607 (j = 8000 after (f-3)/2 steps), and on 44 primes D m^2 - 1 with m not a power of 2, four for each D in {3, 7, 8, 11, 12, 19, 27, 28, 43, 67, 163}. All of them: G cyclic of order m, all j in F_p, none repeated. Table: `results/primes.tsv`.
- Item 4, integers only, all primes p = 3 mod 4 below 3*10^6 inert in Q(sqrt(-D)): 54271, 54274, 54256, 54271, 54271 primes for D = 3, 8, 11, 12, 27 and 54260 for D = 7 and 28. No admissible quadruple lies below the stated minimum, out of about 170 million pairs (c, e) tried. For the 708 primes of the form D m^2 - 1 met, the least delta is m.
- Item 7, minimality by exact enumeration (`results/item7.log`). The crate's `shortest_equivalent` returns the first vector of a constant-time Lagrange-Gauss reduction over Z[i] with a fixed number of rounds and does not check it: it reduces, it proves no minimum. The new test computes, in integers only, the exact minimum of the norm form of a left ideal of O0, which is the least degree of an isogeny from E_1728 to the curve of that ideal. For I = O0 theta + O0 m the minimum is m at all three SQIsign primes and at the six MIKE primes. Acting on I with the class group of every order containing O_D gives the other curves admitting O_D: 1, 2 and 4 classes at the SQIsign primes, 80 at the MIKE primes. Every one is at least m away and only the endpoint is at m. This is the whole minimality of Corollary A at the nine primes, given Onuki's theorem that the class group acts transitively on the curves carrying an order, up to Frobenius. The least degree to the curve of the reduced form (a, b, c) of discriminant -D/g^2 comes out as exactly g a m. The same code agrees with a direct search for the least admissible quadruple on 1886 pairs (D, p) with D <= 100 and p < 1000 (2448 comparisons).

## Proposed "Numerical verification" paragraph

The statements were checked in Rust in the sqisign-rs repository. At the three SQIsign primes the group G was computed from the precomputed action of (iota + pi)/2 on E[2^f], found cyclic of order exactly m, and the isogeny with kernel G was walked on the library's field in three ways that agree (single 2-isogenies, the library's 4-isogeny step, its strategy-based chain): it ends at j = 0 through j = 54000 after 162 steps, at j = -12288000 after 250 steps, and at a root of H_{-68} after 331 steps, every intermediate j-invariant lying in F_p and none repeating. On the quaternion side the left ideal generated by theta and m has norm m, its right order contains theta/m as an optimally embedded element of discriminant -D, and an exact enumeration of its norm form, and of the norm forms of the other ideal classes of curves admitting the order of discriminant -D, shows that no isogeny from E_1728 to any of these curves has degree below m; the same holds at the six MIKE primes. An independent implementation over a generic F_{p^2} reproduces these chains and confirms the kernel and the endpoint for the six MIKE primes, for the fifteen primes 3*2^f - 1 with f even below 500, for the twelve Mersenne primes 2^f - 1 with 5 <= f <= 607, and for 44 primes D m^2 - 1 with m not a power of 2 and h(-D) = 1. In integers, for D in {3, 8, 11, 12, 27} and each of the roughly 54000 primes p = 3 mod 4 below 3*10^6 inert in Q(sqrt(-D)), no admissible quadruple exists below the least delta with D delta^2 - p a square; for D = 7 and 28 none exists below the minimum over the two conditions. The check N1 by modular polynomials and the kernel check over larger extension fields are done in SageMath, separately.
